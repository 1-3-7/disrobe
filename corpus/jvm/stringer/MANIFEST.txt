Why disrobe stays detect-only on Stringer
------------------------------------------
Stringer's runtime decrypt is anti-tamper and stack-trace-keyed: the recovered plaintext
is a function of the calling class+method hashCode (via Thread.currentThread().getStackTrace()),
a lazily-built global Object[] key table, BigInteger[] state, and an LRU cache. Reproducing
it statically would require emulating per-call-site caller identity inside a near-full JVM,
which is out of scope for a static peel pass. disrobe therefore detects and parses the
protector honestly and does NOT fabricate cleartext; the 3 encrypted constants remain opaque.
