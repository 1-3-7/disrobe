InstallShield cabinet fixtures for disrobe-binfmt

Every file here is an unmodified byte copy of an artifact published in the unshield
project's regression corpus (https://github.com/twogood/unshield, MIT). The archives
themselves are third-party 1997-era installer data redistributed by that project; they
are pinned here so the InstallShield walker is graded against real cabinets rather than
against fixtures written from disrobe's own understanding of the layout.

Expected inventories

  <fixture>.tsv holds the reference inventory: index, disposition, carve path, declared
  expanded size, declared compressed size, and the SHA-256 of the recovered bytes.

  The inventories were produced by a reference decoder written directly from unshield's
  published sources (lib/libunshield.c, lib/file.c, lib/directory.c, lib/file_group.c,
  lib/helper.c, lib/cabfile.h at commit cbed3d19ef9cdf4e5fb86b9d65e48f1bf8d539db). That
  reference decoder was graded against unshield's own recorded MD5 inventories before it
  was used here:

    test/v0/the-feeble-files-spanish   319 of 319 members matched (full-flush framing)
    test/v5/CVE-2015-1386              extraction fails, as unshield's suite requires
    baldurs gate patch v1.1.4315       132 of 132 members matched (chunk framing)

  Those two graded archives are 26.7 MB and 13.5 MB of third-party game data and are not
  pinned here. Reproduce the grade with:

    curl -sSLO https://raw.githubusercontent.com/twogood/unshield/main/test/v0/the-feeble-files-spanish/test_data.zip
    curl -sSLO https://raw.githubusercontent.com/twogood/unshield/main/test/v0/the-feeble-files-spanish/the-feeble-files-spanish.md5
    curl -sSLO https://raw.githubusercontent.com/twogood/unshield/main/test/v0/baldurs_gate_patch_v1_1_4315_international/test_data.zip
    curl -sSLO https://raw.githubusercontent.com/twogood/unshield/main/test/v0/baldurs_gate_patch_v1_1_4315_international/baldurs_gate_patch_v1_1_4315_international.md5

  The MD5 lists in those directories are unshield's recorded output for `unshield -O x`
  and `unshield x` respectively, so they are independent of both disrobe and the
  reference decoder.

Carve paths

  unshield's command line writes each member to <file group>/<directory>/<name> and
  replaces space, angle bracket and square bracket characters with underscores. Disrobe
  keeps the same three-component path but does not rewrite characters; path safety is
  decided by the shared entry-path sanitiser instead.
