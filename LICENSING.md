## Crediting Disrobe

This guide implements Section 6 of the Disrobe Source-Available License, Version 1.1. The License controls if this guide and the License differ.

### Required credit

Every Publication arising from use of Disrobe or its Output must contain this exact line:

```text
This work used Disrobe, created by 1-3-7: https://github.com/1-3-7/disrobe
```

The requirement applies to every user and every license category: personal projects, independent research, academic work, nonprofit research, journalism, and commercial use under a paid license. It applies to public, internal, confidential, and client-facing work, and to work that contains no Disrobe code but relies on Output, including translated, summarized, reformatted, or agent-generated material.

Include the credit by the first time the work is circulated, submitted, delivered, or presented to anyone else, and keep it in later versions. Private working notes that are never shared do not need it until they are shared.

The credit identifies the creator of the tool. It does not make 1-3-7 a coauthor, analyst, expert, sponsor, or endorser of your work, and you must not describe it that way. A generic list of tools, a site-wide terms page, a buried code comment, or a link without the creator's name does not satisfy the requirement.

Client preference, editorial preference, and voluntary confidentiality agreements are not legal restrictions. Never disclose protected information to satisfy the credit requirement.
