## Uses that are free

Only the Non-Commercial Use categories in License Section 3.2 are free: an individual's personal hobby projects and personal learning; an individual's own independent security research that is not done for an employer, client, or organization and not undertaken to earn a reward; teaching, coursework, and academic research at nonprofit or public educational institutions; research within a genuine nonprofit's mission that is not a paid service; and Bona Fide Journalism under License Section 5. Anyone may read the source and prepare contributions under License Section 3.5. Free use still requires attribution and compliance with every restriction in the License.

## Requesting a license

Open an issue in the Official Repository titled **Commercial license request** and mention **@1-3-7**, or use another channel the Licensor designates in the Official Repository. Public issues are visible to everyone: state only the general use category, whether you need internal use or client-service rights, the approximate number of users, and that you need a private channel. Do not post targets, credentials, client names, case identifiers, or other confidential information.

Before signing, you will supply through the agreed private channel the legal name of the contracting organization, its authorized signatory, the intended scope, users and deployments, affiliates or contractors to be covered, and the requested term.

## What the signed agreement covers

The agreement identifies the licensee, covered personnel and affiliates, permitted activities and deployment limits, term, fees and payment terms, and any expressly granted redistribution, hosting, or client-service rights. Support, service levels, warranties, or indemnification by the Licensor exist only if the agreement states them expressly.

**Attribution remains required.** Every commercial license keeps the credit requirement in License Section 6 unless the signed agreement expressly waives it:

